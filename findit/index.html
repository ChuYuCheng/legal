FindIt 隱私權政策

FindIt 是本地優先的物品位置記錄 App。你的物品名稱、照片、辨識文字、位置路徑、筆記、搜尋紀錄與 App 設定都只儲存在你的裝置與 App 沙盒中。

FindIt 不建立帳號、不使用廣告 SDK、不追蹤你，也不收集或傳輸任何個人資料到我們的伺服器。照片原圖儲存在 App 沙盒；縮圖與物品資料儲存在本機資料庫。語音查詢使用系統語音辨識，設計上要求在裝置上進行。App 內購買由 Apple 處理；我們不會取得你的付款卡號或 Apple ID 密碼。

FindIt Privacy Policy

FindIt is a local-first app for remembering where your things are. Item names, photos, recognized text, location paths, notes, search history, and app settings are stored only on your device inside the app sandbox.

FindIt does not create accounts, use advertising SDKs, track you, or collect or transmit personal data to our servers. Original photos are stored in the app sandbox; thumbnails and item records are stored in the local database. Voice search uses Apple system speech recognition and is configured to run on device. In-app purchases are handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment card number or Apple ID password.
